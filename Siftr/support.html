技术支持

需要 iOS 18 或以上版本

遇到问题或有功能建议，请发邮件到 imhccn@gmail.com，附上机型、系统版本和「设置」里显示的最后同步时间会更有帮助。

开始之前需要什么

  • 一台 iOS 18 或更新系统的 iPhone 或 iPad。
  • 一个坚果云账号（其他标准 WebDAV 服务也可，但默认预设是坚果云）。
  • 一个由 AI Agent（例如 Hermes）发布到你云盘的内容目录，里面应有 manifest.json 和 Markdown 简报。

首次配置

  • 在坚果云网页「账户信息 → 安全选项 → 第三方应用管理」中生成一个应用密码（为 Siftr 单独创建，不要与发布端共用）。
  • 打开 Siftr，输入服务器地址（默认 https://dav.jianguoyun.com/dav/）、账号邮箱、应用密码和内容目录（默认 Siftr）。
  • 点「测试连接」确认能读到内容后保存，回到首页下拉刷新即可看到简报。

刷新后没有新内容？

先确认发布端当天是否执行成功；Siftr 首页会显示最后同步时间。网络失败或格式异常时，App 会保留上一次的内容并提示错误，不会清空首页；稍后再下拉重试即可。

飞行模式下能阅读吗？

能。凡是打开过的文章都缓存在本机，断网时照常阅读；从未打开过的文章则需要联网下载。

清理缓存会删掉收藏吗？

不会。「设置」里的清理只移除可重新下载的正文缓存；收藏、已读状态和阅读进度都保留。

应用密码换了怎么办？

打开「设置 → 连接坚果云」，输入新密码重新测试并保存；此前已缓存的内容不受影响，照常可读。

文章更新后阅读进度会丢吗？

不会。阅读状态按文档 ID 记录，云端修订正文不会重置你的已读、收藏和阅读位置。

Debug 示例模式

开发版可以在启动参数中加 --demo 查看明确标记的示例简报，其数据与真实配置完全隔离；正式版不含示例数据。

Support

Requires iOS 18 or later

For problems or ideas, email imhccn@gmail.com — including your device model, iOS version, and the last-sync time shown in Settings helps a lot.

What you need to start

  • An iPhone or iPad running iOS 18 or later.
  • A Jianguoyun account (other standard WebDAV services work too; Jianguoyun is the default preset).
  • A content folder in your cloud, published by an AI agent (for example Hermes), containing a manifest.json and Markdown briefings.

First-time setup

  • Generate an app-specific password under Jianguoyun's web settings (Account Info → Security → third-party app management) — create a separate one for Siftr, not shared with your publisher.
  • Open Siftr and enter the server address (default https://dav.jianguoyun.com/dav/), account email, app password, and content folder (default Siftr).
  • Tap "Test Connection", save once it succeeds, then pull to refresh on the home feed.

No new content after refreshing?

First check whether your publishing agent ran today; Siftr's home shows the last sync time. On network or format errors the app keeps the previous content and shows the error instead of emptying the feed — just pull to retry later.

Can I read offline?

Yes. Every article you have opened is cached on the device and readable without a network; articles never opened need a connection to download.

Does clearing the cache delete favorites?

No. Clearing in Settings removes only re-downloadable article bodies; favorites, read states, and reading positions are kept.

Changed the app password?

Open Settings → Jianguoyun connection, enter the new password, test, and save. Previously cached content is unaffected and stays readable.

Will revisions reset my progress?

No. Reading state is bound to each document's stable ID, so a revised body never resets your read state, favorites, or position.

Demo mode

Debug builds accept a --demo launch argument that shows clearly labeled sample briefings in an isolated environment; release builds contain no sample data.
