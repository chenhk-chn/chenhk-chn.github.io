Siftr

AI 负责整理，你负责阅读

Siftr 把 AI Agent 每天发布到坚果云的 Markdown 简报，变成一本安静的原生刊物：连接你自己的 WebDAV，下拉刷新取新内容，正文按需缓存、飞行模式也能接着读；收藏和阅读位置只保存在这台设备上。

适用于 iPhone 与 iPad · 需要 iOS 18 或更新版本

正在 TestFlight 内测参加测试或反馈

  • 连接你自己的云盘首次配置输入 WebDAV 地址、第三方应用密码和内容目录；简报存放在你自己的坚果云里，Siftr 不经手任何第三方服务器。
  • 像刊物，不像网盘首页按时间倒序排列每日简报，最新一期突出显示；标题、摘要、分类与标签一目了然，未读状态清晰可辨。
  • 正文按需缓存打开过的文章保存在本机；再次进入秒开，飞行模式、地铁里都能接着读，网络异常也绝不清空已读内容。
  • 阅读进度不丢已读/未读、滚动位置和最近阅读时间绑定文档本身；云端修订正文或刷新索引，都不会清掉你的记录。
  • 收藏与全文搜索一键收藏值得保留的简报；标题、摘要、标签乃至正文都能搜，搜索结果依然可以离线打开已缓存的文章。
  • 安静地刷新下拉即取最新索引，内容没有变化就不重复下载；同步失败保留旧内容，并明确显示最后成功同步的时间。

没有 Siftr 账号，没有广告，也没有跟踪。你的阅读记录只属于这台设备。

A quiet home for your AI briefings

Siftr turns the Markdown briefings your AI agent publishes to your own WebDAV cloud each day into a calm, native magazine: pull to refresh, articles cached on demand and readable offline, with favorites and reading positions kept on this device alone.

For iPhone and iPad · Requires iOS 18 or later · The app interface currently speaks Simplified Chinese

Now in TestFlightJoin the beta or send feedback

  • Your cloud, your dataSet up once with a WebDAV address, an app-specific password, and a content folder. Briefings live in your own Jianguoyun storage — Siftr operates no server of its own.
  • A magazine, not a file listThe home feed reads like a table of contents: the latest issue up top, then every briefing in reverse chronological order with titles, summaries, categories, and tags.
  • Cached as you readArticles you open stay on the device — instant on revisit, fully readable in airplane mode or on the subway, and never wiped by a failed refresh.
  • Progress that sticksRead state, scroll position, and recents are bound to each document's identity; revisions and index refreshes never reset your place.
  • Favorites and full-text searchKeep the briefings worth keeping, and search titles, summaries, tags, and body text — cached results still open offline.
  • Refreshes quietlyPull to fetch the latest index; unchanged content is never re-downloaded, a failed sync keeps what you had, and the last successful sync time is always shown.

No Siftr account, no ads, no tracking. Your reading history stays on your device.
