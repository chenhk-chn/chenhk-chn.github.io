隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月

Placr 完全在你的 Mac 上运行。它没有服务器，不需要注册账号，开发者也收不到你的任何数据。

不收集的内容

Placr 不会收集、存储或向开发者控制的任何服务器传输个人信息。应用中没有分析统计、没有崩溃上报、没有广告、没有跨应用追踪，也没有集成任何第三方数据收集 SDK。

照片

在你授予照片权限后，Placr 会读取你所选月份照片的拍摄时间、位置信息和缩略图，用来找出缺少位置的照片并按时间分组。这些处理全部在你的 Mac 本机完成，照片本身不会离开你的设备。

只有当你在确认页面明确同意后，Placr 才会把你选择的地点写入照片。每次写入前的原始位置都会记录在本机，你可以随时在操作记录中恢复。Placr 不会删除照片，也不会修改位置以外的任何元数据。

地点搜索

搜索地点时，只有你输入的文字会发送给地图服务。如果当前存在参考坐标，还会附带一个大致的地理范围，用来提高结果相关性。你的照片、照片的位置和其他任何信息都不会被发送。

默认使用 Apple 地图（MapKit）。这部分查询适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

如果你在设置中填入了自己的 Google Maps API Key，并且 Apple 地图没有返回结果，Placr 会转而查询 Google Places API。这部分查询适用 Google 的隐私政策。这个 Key 属于你自己，相关请求也计入你自己的 Google 账户。

本机存储的内容

以下数据保存在 Placr 的沙盒容器内，只有你可以访问，删除应用即随之删除：

  • 操作记录：每次写入了哪些照片、原先的位置是什么
  • 尚未完成的审阅草稿
  • 时区查询缓存
  • 偏好设置：所选月份、界面语言、Dock 图标

你的 Google Maps API Key 保存在 macOS 钥匙串中，不在上述文件里，也不会随草稿或记录一同导出。

儿童

Placr 不面向儿童设计，也不会收集任何与年龄相关的信息。

政策变更

如果本政策发生变化，本页顶部的更新日期会同步修改。涉及重大变更时会在应用内提示。

联系方式

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Placr runs entirely on your Mac. There is no server, no account, and no way for the developer to receive any of your data.

What is not collected

Placr does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to any server controlled by the developer. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no cross-app tracking, and no third-party data collection SDK of any kind.

Photos

Once you grant photo access, Placr reads the capture time, location, and thumbnail of the photos in the month you selected, in order to find the ones missing a location and group them by time. All of this happens on your Mac. Your photos never leave your device.

Placr writes a location to a photo only after you explicitly approve it on the confirmation screen. The original location is recorded locally before every write, so you can restore it at any time from the operation history. Placr never deletes photos and never modifies metadata other than location.

Place search

When you search for a place, only the text you typed is sent to the map service. If a reference coordinate is available, an approximate geographic area is included to improve relevance. Your photos, their locations, and any other information are not sent.

Apple Maps (MapKit) is used by default. Those queries are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you have entered your own Google Maps API key in Settings and Apple Maps returns no result, Placr queries the Google Places API instead. Those queries are covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The key is yours, and the requests are billed to your own Google account.

What is stored on your Mac

The following is kept inside Placr's sandbox container, accessible only to you, and removed when you delete the app:

  • Operation history: which photos were written and what their locations were before
  • Review drafts you have not finished
  • A cache of time zone lookups
  • Preferences: selected month, interface language, Dock icon

Your Google Maps API key is stored in the macOS Keychain, separately from the files above, and is never included in exported drafts or history.

Children

Placr is not directed to children and does not collect any age-related information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Significant changes will also be surfaced in the app.
