Placr

给「照片」里缺少位置的照片补上地点 · macOS 15+

Placr 按月份扫描你的照片图库，把缺少位置的照片按拍摄时间分组，参考同一时段有位置的照片给出建议，并在你确认后批量写入。原位置始终可恢复。

Placr scans your photo library one month at a time, groups the photos missing a location by capture time, suggests a place based on nearby photos that do have one, and writes them in bulk once you confirm. Every write can be undone.
